Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade my membership? +
After creating your account, go to your dashboard and click "Membership". Select your desired tier and complete payment via Stripe. Your upgraded listing will be live within minutes.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your membership will remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which it will revert to Free.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) via Stripe. All payments are processed securely and discretely.
How does the annual plan work? +
With the annual plan, you pay for 12 months upfront at a 20% discount. This is the best value if you're committed to building your presence on kiki.com.
